# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Roddy Taing and Keith Levin for developing a careful account of how embedding-dimension misspecification changes spectral-network estimation. The separation between under-specification, over-specification, correct specification, and noise-eigenvector behavior gives independent auditors a useful structure for checking assumptions and failure modes.

This repository is an independent clean-room audit. It keeps the paper authors and their claims clearly attributed, records the finite evidence that could be checked, and leaves the failed C2 proxy visible rather than turning it into an unsupported theorem conclusion.
